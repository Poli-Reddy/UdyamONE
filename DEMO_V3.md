# UdyamOne judge demo — preload runbook and final script

The official video must remain **under three minutes**. Target **2:50–2:55**.

This demo has one purpose: make the judges understand UdyamOne immediately, prove every module requested in the brief, and leave them remembering the three decisions that make it different.

## What the judges must understand first

Before showing forms or features, establish three things:

1. **What it is:** UdyamOne is Airtel Business's decision-and-recovery layer for small businesses, covering the journey from needs to activation.
2. **Its speciality:** it protects decision integrity across the lifecycle—it can avoid an unnecessary sale, preserve completed work after a failure, and avoid assistance that has no modeled value.
3. **What will be proved:** Advisor, transparent Quote and Checkout, Smart KYC, and Order Tracker—the four modules requested in the brief.

Do not begin with technology, test counts, architecture or a feature list. Begin with Joseph and the business problem.

# Delivery guidance

- Look at the screen only when pointing to evidence; otherwise speak as if talking directly to one judge.
- Emphasize **not to sell**, **stays complete**, and **contacts nobody**. These are the three memory anchors.
- Do not read every price or label. Say the insight while the screen proves the detail.
- Avoid “our platform has.” Prefer “Joseph sees,” “the system keeps,” or “nothing changes until he accepts.”
- Do not rush silence. A short pause after each result feels confident and lets judges absorb it.
- Keep music absent or very low. Clear narration matters more.
- Use only the five short captions defined in the timeline.

## Truth boundaries

Never say:

- live Airtel provisioning;
- production or legally certified KYC;
- measured uplift;
- guaranteed savings;
- production-ready.

Say instead:

- catalogue-constrained planner;
- local reference-record document checks;
- dependency and evidence workflow;
- priced recovery requiring customer acceptance;
- uplift-ready randomized pilot design;
- production-shaped secure pilot foundation.
